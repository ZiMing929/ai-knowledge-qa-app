Jing'an Day Trip Itinerary: From Shanghai Maritime University

Designed for students and explorers -- optimized for subway, time, and fun!

---

Starting Point: Shanghai Maritime University (Lingang Campus)
- Address: 1550 Haigang Avenue, Pudong New Area
- Nearest Metro: Lingang Dadao Station (Line 16)
- How to get there:
  - Walk ~10 minutes from campus gate to station (follow signs to "Lingang Dadao Station")
  - Or take Bus 1077 (5-minute ride, 1 stop) from "Shanghai Maritime University Stop" to Lingang Dadao Station (Exit 1)

---

Leg 1: Lingang Campus -> Jing'an Dajue City (Zhongxing Road Station)
- Total time: ~85 minutes (including walking and waiting)
- Route:
  1. Line 16 (toward Longyang Road) -> Longyang Road Station (12 stops, ~35 minutes)
  2. Transfer to Line 7 (toward Meilan Lake) -> Jing'an Temple Station (7 stops, ~20 minutes)
  3. Transfer to Line 12 (toward Qixin Road) -> Zhongxing Road Station (2 stops, ~5 minutes)
- Arrive at Dajue City: Exit 1 or 2 -> <1 minute walk to mall entrance
- Recommended arrival time: 10:45 AM

Tip: Use Alipay or WeChat Metro QR code for ¥0.20 discount per ride.

---

At Jing'an Dajue City (10:45 - 15:30)
- Highlights:
  - Rooftop Sky Park: China's first rooftop Ferris wheel (open 10:00-22:00, ¥30)
  - Anime Zone (B1-B2): Genshin Impact pop-ups, figure stores, themed cafés
  - Food: "Da Yue Noodle House" (dan dan mian), "Mao's Dumpling" (xiao long bao)
  - Best photo spot: Glass skybridge between towers (3F)

---

Leg 2: Dajue City -> Global Harbor (Zhongshan Park Station)
- Total time: ~20 minutes
- Route:
  - Line 8 (toward Shendu Highway) -> People's Square Station (2 stops, ~4 minutes)
  - Transfer inside station to Line 2 (toward Guanglan Road) -> Zhongshan Park Station (4 stops, ~10 minutes)
- Arrive at Global Harbor: Exit 4 -> directly into mall atrium
- Expected arrival: ~16:00

---

At Global Harbor (16:00 - 21:00)
- Highlights:
  - Indoor European Garden (B1): Waterfalls, rose arches
  - Luxury Row: Coach, Michael Kors, Swarovski
  - Food Court (5F): "Xiao Yang Run Bing", "Jin Jiang Dumplings"
  - Art Corridors: Rotating exhibitions in central dome (free entry)

Tip: Mall open until 22:00; great for evening bubble tea at "Hey Tea" (3F).

---

Return Journey (Optional)
- From Zhongshan Park -> Lingang: Same route reversed (Line 2 -> Line 7 -> Line 16)
- Last Line 16 train from Longyang Road: ~22:40 -> arrives Lingang ~23:30

---

Budget Summary (per person)
- Metro fare (round-trip): ¥8.5
- Rooftop Ferris wheel: ¥30
- Lunch + snacks: ¥60-100
- Souvenir / small buy: ¥50-150
- Total estimate: ¥150-300

---

Ideal Day Plan
- 09:15: Leave campus -> Lingang Dadao Station
- 10:45: Arrive Dajue City -- rooftop & photos
- 12:30: Lunch at food court
- 14:00: Explore anime zone & shops
- 15:30: Depart for Global Harbor
- 16:00: Arrive -- garden & luxury stroll
- 18:30: Dinner & dessert
- 20:30: Wind down at café, prepare return
